# FE-Radar Product Launch Video Script

Date: 2026-06-28
Target: product launch / internal introduction meeting
Length: 80-90 seconds
Format: 1920x1080, with optional 1080x1920 cutdown

## Creative Direction

Theme:

> 从噪音到信号，让风险先到。

Tone:

- enterprise technology, not consumer AI
- calm, credible, operational
- Far East blue and cyan as primary palette
- real product UI as proof

Design guardrail:

Do not recreate the earlier starfield montage. This version should look like a Far East internal product launch: real industry context, real UI, large panels, precise motion, restrained highlights.

## Scene Script

### 01. Opening: Industry Noise

Time: 0:00-0:08

Visual:

Deep Far East blue-black background. Industry signals appear as faint layers: policy, tender, copper, lithium, competitor, accident, capacity, standard. They move quickly, then compress into a clean signal line.

On-screen text:

```text
每天，产业信息都在加速。
```

Voiceover:

```text
每天，电力、电缆、储能、铜锂行情和竞品动态，都在不断刷新。
```

Motion:

No random starfield. Use structured document strips, source labels, and data lines.

### 02. Problem: Important Signals Are Buried

Time: 0:08-0:16

Visual:

One risk card rises from the information flow, then is almost buried by more updates. A red edge line marks the cost of missing it.

On-screen text:

```text
真正重要的，可能只有几条。
```

Voiceover:

```text
对管理、市场、采购和品牌团队来说，错过一条，可能就是一次机会，或一场风险。
```

### 03. Reveal: FE-Radar

Time: 0:16-0:24

Visual:

Use `brand/fareast-logo-white.png`. A restrained radar sweep reveals the product name. Background uses generated command center image with very low opacity, then transitions to real UI.

On-screen text:

```text
FE-Radar
远东控股产业情报雷达
```

Voiceover:

```text
这就是 FE-Radar，远东控股的产业情报雷达。
```

### 04. Product Philosophy: Source First

Time: 0:24-0:36

Visual:

Use `generated/02-source-risk-radar.png` as background. Overlay source tiers: T1 government and exchange, T2 industry media, T3 supplementary sources. Show C1/C2/C3 attention circles.

On-screen text:

```text
信源比信息重要
T1 权威源 · T2 行业源 · T3 补充源
```

Voiceover:

```text
FE-Radar 的第一条原则是，信源比信息重要。先选对源头，再处理信息。
```

### 05. Product Philosophy: Rules First

Time: 0:36-0:48

Visual:

Show pipeline blocks: fetch, prefilter, NER, scorer, embedder, cluster, curator, daily. Keep the animation precise and mechanical. Only language tasks glow lightly.

On-screen text:

```text
能用代码，就不交给猜测
规则 · 阈值 · 聚类 · 告警
```

Voiceover:

```text
第二条原则是克制。规则、阈值、聚类和告警由代码控制，AI 只做语言理解和总结。
```

### 06. Product Proof: Real UI

Time: 0:48-1:02

Visual:

Make `existing/ui/01-dashboard.png` the hero screen. Camera moves slowly across the dashboard, then cuts to `03-detail.png` and `04-digest.png`. Use large readable panels, not a floating screenshot collage.

On-screen text:

```text
时间线 · 精选 · 日报
每 6 小时自动更新
```

Voiceover:

```text
它把分散的信息整理成时间线、精选内容和每日简报，每六小时自动更新。
```

### 07. Business Scene: Copper And Lithium Briefing

Time: 1:02-1:14

Visual:

Use `generated/03-commodity-briefing-desk.png` as context. Overlay copper and lithium briefing cards. Show report delivery to internal users.

On-screen text:

```text
铜 · 锂行情简报
采购、成本、报价的共同语言
```

Voiceover:

```text
对于铜和锂这样的关键原料，它还可以形成行情简报，服务采购、成本判断和销售报价。
```

### 08. Alert: About Far East, Zero Blind Spots

Time: 1:14-1:24

Visual:

Far East node is highlighted in the C1 attention circle. Related mentions become traceable alert lines. Use cyan for normal signal, red only for risk.

On-screen text:

```text
关于远东，风险不漏
```

Voiceover:

```text
尤其是关于远东自己的动态，系统用实体命中和规则计算保障优先级，减少遗漏。
```

### 09. Close: Risk Arrives First

Time: 1:24-1:30

Visual:

Clean final lockup. White logo, product name, slogan. No extra particles.

On-screen text:

```text
FE-Radar
信源驱动，风险先到
```

Voiceover:

```text
FE-Radar。信源驱动，风险先到。
```

## Delivery Notes

- Use real UI frames as the visual proof.
- Generated images only provide business environment and atmosphere.
- Keep every on-screen sentence under 14 Chinese characters when possible.
- Use one motion idea per scene.
- BGM should be restrained corporate electronic, with subtle UI ticks and sweep sounds.
