# FE-Radar Brand Kit

Date: 2026-06-28
Scope: product launch video and presentation assets

## Product Position

FE-Radar is Far East Holding Group's internal industrial intelligence radar for power, cable, energy storage, copper, lithium, competitors, customers, and policy signals.

The product should feel like an operational command system, not a generic AI SaaS demo.

## Typography

Use system fonts for reliable rendering:

- Chinese and UI: `PingFang SC`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `system-ui`
- Numeric and dashboard labels: `JetBrains Mono`, `IBM Plex Mono`, `Menlo`, `ui-monospace`

Avoid display fonts with decorative curves. FE-Radar should read as precise, internal, and reliable.

## Visual Direction

Use:

- enterprise blue-white operating surfaces
- real UI as the main subject
- radar scan lines tied to real signals
- source network and pipeline diagrams grounded in FE-Radar logic
- restrained cyan highlights
- large readable interface panels

Avoid:

- random star fields
- purple neon gradients as the dominant look
- generic bokeh or abstract "AI particles"
- small unreadable screenshots floating as decoration
- white logo pills pasted into dark scenes
- fake unsupported KPIs
- magic-agent claims that bypass the product's rule-based design

## Design Warnings

1. Lead with business context, not decoration.
   The viewer should know this is an industrial intelligence product for Far East within the first 5 seconds.

2. Use one strong product screen at a time.
   Multi-card montages quickly become unreadable. Use large panels and move the camera through them.

3. Do not turn "AI" into the main character.
   FE-Radar's design philosophy is "source first" and "rules first." LLMs support language work; code controls scoring, thresholds, clustering, and alerts.

4. Treat commodity briefing as a business scene.
   Copper and lithium pricing should appear as decision support for procurement, cost, and sales, not as generic finance charts.

5. Preserve the compliance tone.
   Mention internal deployment, source traceability, privacy scrubber, and no raw HTML snapshot storage when relevant. Do not imply public scraping without boundaries.
